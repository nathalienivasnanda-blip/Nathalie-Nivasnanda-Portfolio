About Nathalie Nivasnanda

Welcome, my name is Nathalie (pronounced like "Natalie"). I'm a Technical Writer and Documentation Quality Analyst based in the US. Getting my start as a Technical Writer in December 2020, I've worked with a number of different clients primarily in the technology world doing all things documentation related. From working on user guides, manuals, standard operating procedures, processflow diagrams, reports, fliers, and more, you'll likely find me behind a computer screen writing, playing around with a new tool or reading a book!

I have an educational background in Writing, Rhetoric and Discourse where I obtained both my Bachelors and Masters of Arts in Writing, Rhetoric and Discourse from DePaul University.

I can be contacted via my LinkedIn. or email at nathalie.nivasnanda@gmail.com.
